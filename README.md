# My Favorite Anime

## Haikyu!!

**Haikyu!!** is an anime about a short but determined boy who joins his school's volleyball team and grows with his teammates to reach the nationals.

### Why I Like It
- Exciting matches
- Fun and well-developed characters
- Inspiring and emotional story

### Main Characters

3. Tobio Kageyama
